# Open Questions

| Question | Why it matters | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product idea: What product do you want to build in one sentence? | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
| Target users: Who is the first user group this product should serve? | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
| Pain points: What is the sharpest user pain or scenario? | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
| Current workaround: How do users solve this problem today? | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
| Core workflow: What core workflow takes users from entry to value? | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
| MVP must-haves: List the v1 must-haves — at most 3. More than 3 means you have not prioritized. | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
| The one thing: If you could ship only ONE of those, which one proves the idea? | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
| Non-goals: Name at least one thing you are deliberately NOT doing in v1 (and why). | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
| Data model: What data must the product record or generate? | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
| Deterministic rules: Which calculations or decisions must be deterministic rather than AI-generated? | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
| AI boundaries: What should AI do, and what should AI never decide by itself? | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
| Trust mechanism: What evidence, sources, or state should be shown behind AI output? | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
| Risks: What privacy, safety, permission, or misleading-output risks matter? | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
| Primary success metric: One measurable signal that v1 worked (a single number or observable result). | Left blank in PRD interview | open |
